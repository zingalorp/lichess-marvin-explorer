MARVIN OPENING EXPLORER - WINDOWS SETUP

Works on 64-bit Windows 10/11 with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
Everything it needs is included: no Python, no extra downloads, no admin rights.

1. Right-click the ZIP and choose "Extract All".
   Put the folder somewhere you will keep it, such as Documents.

2. Open the extracted folder and double-click Setup.cmd.
   Wait for "Setup complete!" (this can take a minute).

3. Follow the steps Setup shows to add the extension to your browser.
   Press Enter in the Setup window and it opens the extensions page for you.
   In short: turn on "Developer mode", click "Load unpacked" and choose the
   "extension" folder inside the extracted folder.

4. Open https://lichess.org/analysis, click the book button under the board
   and choose the "Marvin" tab.

Marvin also works on finished games and in studies. The gear button sets the
rating and time control. The extension's toolbar icon (pin it from the
puzzle-piece menu) chooses the model and display options.

IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK

- "Host not found": run Setup.cmd again, restart the browser, reload Lichess.
- No Marvin tab: make sure the extension is turned on, click its reload
  button on the extensions page, then reload Lichess and open the book panel.
- "Folder path is too long": move the folder somewhere shorter, such as
  Documents\Marvin, then run Setup.cmd again.
- Moved the folder? Run Setup.cmd again, remove the extension on the
  extensions page and load the "extension" folder from its new place.

TO REMOVE IT

Double-click Uninstall.cmd, remove Marvin Opening Explorer on the browser's
extensions page, then delete the folder.

The model runs on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
